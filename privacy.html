Privacy Notice

This Privacy Notice explains how the accommodation operator collects and uses information when you register to access the complimentary guest Wi-Fi.

In short:

We ask for your email address so that we can maintain a record of guest Wi-Fi access and help monitor the security and appropriate use of the network.

You can separately choose whether you would like to receive occasional emails about future stays, availability and direct-booking opportunities. This marketing choice is optional and does not affect your access to Wi-Fi.

1. Who operates the accommodation?

The accommodation and guest Wi-Fi service are operated by the accommodation operator.

If you need to contact us about this Privacy Notice or how your information is used, please use the contact details provided by the accommodation operator.

2. What information do we collect?

When you register for guest Wi-Fi, we may collect:

  • Your email address
  • The property you are staying at
  • The date and time of your Wi-Fi registration
  • Your selected marketing preference
  • The device name you provide during registration

We aim to collect only information that is reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this notice.

3. Why do we collect your email address?

Your email address is required to access our complimentary guest Wi-Fi. We collect it to maintain a record of guest Wi-Fi access and to help monitor the security and appropriate use of our network.

This Wi-Fi registration purpose is separate from marketing. You do not have to agree to receive marketing emails in order to access the guest Wi-Fi.

4. Marketing communications

Marketing is optional. If you actively choose to receive marketing communications, we may use your email address to send occasional information about:

  • Future stays
  • Availability
  • Direct-booking opportunities
  • Direct-booking rates
  • Special offers or promotions
  • Invitations to stay again

Your marketing choice is recorded separately from the fact that you registered for Wi-Fi. Choosing not to receive marketing does not prevent you from using the guest Wi-Fi service.

Marketing communications will include an appropriate way to unsubscribe. You can change your mind and stop receiving marketing communications at any time.

5. How we use your information

Information collected through the Wi-Fi registration portal may be used to:

  • Provide access to guest Wi-Fi
  • Maintain a record of Wi-Fi registrations
  • Help monitor network security and appropriate use
  • Respond to requests or questions relating to the Wi-Fi service
  • Record and respect your marketing preference
  • Send marketing communications where you have chosen to receive them

6. How long do we keep your information?

We retain Wi-Fi registration information only for as long as it is reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including network security and record-keeping.

Where you have chosen to receive marketing communications, we may retain your marketing preference and information needed to maintain a record of your choice and respect future unsubscribe requests.

7. Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data protection law gives you rights in relation to your personal information. These may include the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information
  • Ask for inaccurate information to be corrected
  • Request deletion of information where applicable
  • Object to certain uses of your information
  • Request restriction of processing in certain circumstances
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

8. Marketing choices

You can change your mind about receiving marketing communications at any time. The simplest way to do this is to use the unsubscribe option provided in our marketing emails.

Choosing not to receive marketing communications does not affect your ability to use the guest Wi-Fi service.

9. Questions or concerns

If you have questions about this Privacy Notice or how your information is handled, please contact the accommodation operator using the contact details provided by the accommodation.

10. Complaints

If you are concerned about how your personal information has been handled, you can raise your concern with us first.

You also have the right to complain to the UK's Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).
