Guest WiFi

Privacy Notice

This Privacy Notice explains how the accommodation operator collects and uses information when you register to access the complimentary guest WiFi.

In short:

We ask for your email address so that we can maintain a record of guest WiFi access and help monitor the security and appropriate use of the network.

1. Who operates the accommodation?

The accommodation and guest WiFi service are operated by the accommodation operator.

If you need to contact us about this Privacy Notice or how your information is used, please use the contact details provided by the accommodation operator.

2. What information do we collect?

When you register for guest WiFi, we may collect:

  • Your email address
  • The property you are staying at
  • The date and time of your WiFi registration
  • Your selected marketing preference
  • Information about the device you provide during registration

We aim to collect only information that is reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this notice.

3. Why do we collect your email address?

Your email address is required to access our complimentary guest WiFi. We collect this information to maintain a record of guest WiFi access and to help monitor the security and appropriate use of our network.

Your email address is not required to receive marketing emails. Marketing is optional.

4. Marketing communications

If you actively choose to receive marketing communications, we may use your email address to send occasional information about:

  • Future stays
  • Availability
  • Direct-booking opportunities
  • Special rates and promotions
  • Invitations to stay again

You do not have to agree to marketing in order to access guest WiFi.

Every marketing email will provide an appropriate way to unsubscribe or otherwise stop receiving marketing communications.

5. How we use your information

Information collected through the WiFi registration portal may be used to:

  • Provide access to guest WiFi
  • Maintain a record of WiFi registrations
  • Help monitor network security and appropriate use
  • Respond to requests or questions relating to the WiFi service
  • Send marketing communications where you have chosen to receive them

6. Sharing your information

We may use trusted service providers to operate the WiFi registration service, store registration information, host the website and support our business operations.

These providers may process information on our behalf and are expected to handle it appropriately and securely.

7. International transfers

Some service providers used to operate our systems may process information outside the United Kingdom. Where applicable, we will take appropriate steps to ensure that personal information receives protection required by applicable data protection law.

8. How long do we keep your information?

We retain WiFi registration information only for as long as it is reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including network security and record-keeping.

Where you have chosen to receive marketing communications, we may retain your marketing preference and suppression information so that we can respect your choice and avoid sending unwanted marketing.

9. Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data protection law gives you rights in relation to your personal information. These may include the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information
  • Ask for inaccurate information to be corrected
  • Request deletion of information where applicable
  • Object to certain uses of your information
  • Request restriction of processing in certain circumstances
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

10. Marketing choices

You can change your mind about receiving marketing communications at any time. You can use the unsubscribe option provided in our marketing emails.

Choosing not to receive marketing communications does not prevent you from using the guest WiFi service.

11. Questions or concerns

If you have questions about this Privacy Notice or how your information is handled, please contact the accommodation operator using the contact details provided by the accommodation.

12. Complaints

If you are concerned about how your personal information has been handled, you can raise your concern with us first.

You also have the right to complain to the UK's Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).
